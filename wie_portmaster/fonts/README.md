# MiniMobile Sans

The face the SDL frontend draws its own screens with - the game list, the
menus, the settings - at the window's own resolution. The games themselves
keep their handset fonts (`fonts/` at the top of the repository).

It is [Pretendard](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard) by Kil
Hyung-jin, cut down to what those screens need: ASCII, the 2,350 Hangul
syllables of KS X 1001, the Hangul compatibility jamo (for initial-consonant
search) and a few symbols. A syllable outside those is drawn from neodgm.
Pretendard reserves its name, so the cut-down copy is called MiniMobile Sans.
It is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, in `OFL.txt` with Pretendard's
copyright.
